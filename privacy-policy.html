1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GPS CLASS ATTENDANCE.jkuat ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information from users. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name and email address collected during account registration.
  • Location Data: GPS location is collected when the App is in use to verify physical attendance at a class venue.
  • Usage Data: Attendance records, enrolled units, and session history.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify and record class attendance using GPS coordinates.
  • To authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • To allow lecturers to view attendance records for their units.
  • To send notifications related to attendance and class sessions.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. Location data is only captured at the moment of attendance marking and is not tracked continuously in the background.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only accessible to:

  • The registered user themselves.
  • Lecturers managing units the user is enrolled in (attendance records only).
  • Firebase (Google) as our secure backend provider.

6. Location Permission

The App requests access to your device's precise location (GPS) solely to verify attendance. You can deny location permission, but you will not be able to mark attendance. Location is not collected in the background.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for university students and staff aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
